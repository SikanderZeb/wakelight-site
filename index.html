WAKELIGHT

Arrive with help, spend them, walk out alone.

Every room is dark. Light it, and you lose the help that got you there.

Hold a key and the game records a few seconds of your movement. Let go, and you snap back to where you started — while a shadow of you replays exactly what you just did. It's solid. You can stand on it. It can hold down a plate you can't reach from where you need to be.

The catch: your shadows only exist in the dark. Light a lamp and one of them is gone — the oldest first. Every room is the same trade.

37 rooms across seven chapters. Hand-painted. Full controller support.
